A locked-off camera: no camera movement, no zoom, no cut. A shot from a needle-felted stop-motion animation film: a handmade felt and cardboard city diorama late at night, a deep blue sky, two small felt clouds.

The city is going to sleep. For the first second nothing moves. Then, one by one, the few lit windows left across the city switch off, a second or two apart, each with a small soft blink: first the two small windows high in the dark tower at the back left, then the one low on the far left, then the faint ones further back. The two felt clouds drift very slowly to the right. In the middle building, the one warm window with the little desk lamp stays lit the whole time, glowing steadily. By the end it is the only lit window in the whole city.

No people. The buildings, water towers and rooftops never move or change shape; only the lights and the clouds change. Each puppet and object stays exactly the same in every frame: same shape, size, colours and felt. Handmade stop-motion movement, tactile needle-felt fibers, fine fuzz, soft shallow depth of field. No text, no letters, no numbers; any screen shows only a soft glow.
