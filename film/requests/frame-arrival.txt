A still from a needle-felted stop-motion animation short film, cinematic 16:9, shot on a macro lens in a handmade tabletop diorama.

The same miniature felt office at night: warm brass desk lamp, cool laptop glow, a tiny felt city at blue dusk through the window. On the top edge of the small open laptop screen rests a palm-sized white needle-felted cat: it is exactly the cat in reference images 2 and 3 — ivory, very fine loose wool fibers, flat round green felt eyes with black pupils, tiny pink felt nose, pale pink inner ears. Its body is hidden behind the screen; only its head and its two small white front paws show, the paws hanging softly over the screen's top edge. The cat looks down at the woman at the desk.

The woman is exactly the character in reference image 1 — curly black wool hair, cream felt headphones around her neck, terracotta felt skin, blue felt collar — a complete small felt puppet. She has just looked up from the screen at the cat, surprised, the first hint of a smile beginning.

Tactile needle-felt fibers, handmade imperfections, soft shallow depth of field with the cat and her face in focus, gentle film grain, tender and quiet mood, Laika and Aardman stop-motion craft. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI on the screen (the screen shows only a soft glow).
