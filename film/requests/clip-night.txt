A locked-off camera: no camera movement, no zoom, no cut. A shot from a needle-felted stop-motion animation film, in a handmade tabletop diorama late at night. The desk lamp is off; the blue city glows in the window behind.

The small white felt cat is asleep on the top edge of the dark laptop screen, chin on its paws, breathing slowly. For the first two seconds nothing else moves.

Then the laptop screen lights up softly with a cool white glow. The cat's ears twitch; it slowly opens its large green felt eyes and lifts its head a little, looking straight at the camera, calm and warm, and holds still.

The cat is the same character in every frame: compact, groomed ivory felt, large matte green felt eyes with black pupils, a small pink felt nose. It never changes size, shape or position. Only the cat and the screen change. Handmade stop-motion movement, tactile felt fibers. No text, no letters.
