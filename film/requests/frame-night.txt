A still from a needle-felted stop-motion animation short film, cinematic 16:9, shot on a macro lens in a handmade tabletop diorama.

Night in a miniature felt office. A felt puppet woman sits alone at her desk: she is exactly the character in reference image 1 — the same curly black wool hair, cream felt headphones resting on her neck, terracotta felt skin, rounded felt nose and blue felt collar — now as a complete small puppet with a soft felt sweater and felt hands. Her chin rests on one hand; she looks at a small open laptop made of felt, card and wood, whose cool screen glow lights her face. A warm brass desk lamp makes a small pool of amber light on the desk. Behind her, two empty felt office chairs and a dark meeting room. Through a big window, a tiny felt city at night: soft blue dusk, a few warm lit windows, felt clouds.

Tactile needle-felt fibers everywhere, fine fuzz and handmade imperfections, soft shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, quiet and slightly lonely mood, in the craft tradition of Laika and Aardman stop-motion. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI on the screen (the screen shows only a soft glow).
