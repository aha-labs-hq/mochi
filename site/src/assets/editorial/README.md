# Mochi editorial illustrations

Created on 2026-11-01 with the built-in image_gen tool for the SuperAgent editorial. These are conceptual illustrations, not customer photographs or product screenshots. Existing Aha Labs artwork served only as material/palette and Mochi identity references: `controlApp/src/ui/assets/introduction/welcome/project-folio.webp` and `controlApp/Resources/MochiCompanion.png`.

- `shared-context.webp`: colleagues point at one shared project notebook, with Mochi at its edge. One blue thread connects discussion, a confirmed decision and project tasks. Warm cream paper, matte felt, restrained cobalt and amber accents, no text or logos. Website source; its JPEG share derivative lives at `site/public/images/mochi-shared-context.jpg`.
- `continuity.webp`: two felt speech bubbles, an amber confirmation mark and an open project notebook connected by one continuous cobalt thread. Mochi stays beside the notebook. Quiet cream background, generous space, no text or logos.

The original PNGs and complete generation prompts are retained outside the repository in the local publication evidence. WebP masters preserve the generated composition; the existing build pipeline creates responsive AVIF/WebP sizes. Both illustrations keep the established character identity and do not replace the App's approved motion artwork.
